Privacy Policy

The Sudoku Generator app by publisher ElEm Software (Leszek Matuszczyk).

Last updated: 19 September 2026.

Contact: elemsoftware1@gmail.com

What the app does

Sudoku Generator is a puzzle game. Boards, progress, statistics, and remaining hints are stored locally on the device (Android DataStore). The app does not create accounts, does not log you in, and has no in-app purchases.

What we do not collect ourselves

ElEm Software does not run its own analytics server, does not sell user data, and does not require a name, email address, or phone number to play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob when the user wants to refill hints. Google and its advertising partners may process, among other things:

  • the device advertising ID (Advertising ID),
  • approximate location (based on IP address),
  • device information and approximate app-usage data,
  • diagnostic data related to serving the ad.

This data goes to Google, not to ElEm Software servers. Details: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data information.

Consent (EEA / United Kingdom)

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app may show a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. You can decline personalized ads in that message or limit the Advertising ID in Android settings.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by AdMob, not by our own analytics.

The app does not need location, microphone, camera, contacts, or SMS.

Children

The app is not directed at children (it is not in the Designed for Families program). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Android backups

The system may archive local game progress in a device / Google account backup. That is an Android mechanism, not our server.

Changes

We will publish changes to this policy at the same URL and update the date at the top of the page.
